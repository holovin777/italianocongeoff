# Exercise 19.2: Agreeing and Disagreeing 🤝✍️

You now know how to say more than:

> *Sì.*

and:

> *No.*

Excellent.

Civilization has advanced. 😄

In this exercise, you'll practice how to:

- agree strongly;
- agree partially;
- disagree politely;
- express doubt;
- react naturally;
- choose the right tone.

# How Did It Go? 🎯

If you can now disagree without using:

> *No.*

for every situation, you're doing well.

The next step is to make your opinion stronger by explaining:

> **why?**

Because:

> *Secondo me è meglio.*

is an opinion.

But:

> *Secondo me è meglio perché costa meno, consuma meno e dura di più.*

is an argument.

And that's where things get interesting. 😄
