# Expressing Opinions and Arguments 💬🇮🇹

![Two people having a conversation](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1529156069898-49953e39b3ac?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1600&q=80)

At beginner level, you can say:

> *Mi piace.*

At intermediate level:

> *Mi piace molto perché è interessante.*

At advanced level, things become more dangerous:

> *Secondo me hai ragione, però dipende dalla situazione.*

Congratulations.

You now have an **opinion**. 😄

And in Italy, having an opinion is an essential survival skill.

Coffee.

Football.

Politics.

Pasta.

How much pasta should be cooked.

Whether pineapple belongs on pizza.

Especially pineapple on pizza. 🍍🍕

---

## What Will You Learn? 🎯

In this topic, you’ll learn how to:

- express your opinion naturally;
- ask other people what they think;
- agree with someone;
- disagree politely;
- soften a disagreement;
- explain your reasons;
- give examples;
- defend your point of view;
- connect several ideas in one conversation.

The goal is not to win every argument.

The goal is to be able to lose an argument **in Italian**. 😄

## Useful Expressions 🗣️

Here are some expressions you’ll meet during these lessons:

| Italian | English |
|---|---|
| **Secondo me...** | In my opinion... |
| **A mio parere...** | In my view... |
| **Per quanto mi riguarda...** | As far as I'm concerned... |
| **Penso che...** | I think that... |
| **Credo che...** | I believe that... |
| **Sono d'accordo.** | I agree. |
| **Hai ragione.** | You're right. |
| **Non sono d'accordo.** | I disagree. |
| **Non sono del tutto d'accordo.** | I don't completely agree. |
| **Capisco cosa vuoi dire, però...** | I understand what you mean, but... |
| **Dipende.** | It depends. |
| **Il punto è che...** | The point is that... |
| **Il problema è che...** | The problem is that... |
| **Per esempio...** | For example... |
| **Infatti...** | In fact / Indeed... |

# A Very Serious Discussion ☕️

Geoff and Viktor are discussing Italian coffee.

This can only end well.

**Geoff:** Secondo me, il caffè italiano è troppo piccolo.

**Viktor:** Non sono d'accordo. È piccolo perché è molto concentrato.

**Geoff:** Sì, però finisce in tre secondi.

**Viktor:** Questo è vero.

**Geoff:** Quindi ho ragione.

**Viktor:** Non esageriamo.

**Geoff:** A mio parere, dovrebbero servire almeno mezzo litro.

**Viktor:** Geoff, quello non è più un espresso. È una piscina. 😄

# Lesson 19.1 – Giving Your Opinion 💭

Sometimes you simply want to say what you think.

Instead of always using:

> *Penso...*

you’ll learn several natural alternatives:

- *Secondo me...*
- *A mio parere...*
- *Per me...*
- *Per quanto mi riguarda...*
- *Penso che...*
- *Credo che...*

You’ll also discover that these expressions are **not always interchangeable**.

Some are more casual.

Some are more formal.

And some can introduce the famous **congiuntivo**, which is hiding nearby and waiting for Topic 21. 👀

# Lesson 19.2 – Agreeing and Disagreeing 🤝

Saying:

> *No.*

is certainly efficient.

But perhaps not always ideal. 😄

In this lesson, you’ll learn how to agree and disagree naturally:

- *Sono d'accordo.*
- *Hai ragione.*
- *Esatto.*
- *È vero.*
- *Non sono d'accordo.*
- *Non proprio.*
- *Non ne sono così sicuro.*
- *Capisco il tuo punto di vista, però...*

You’ll also learn how to make disagreement sound **less aggressive**.

Very useful when discussing food with Italians.

# Lesson 19.3 – Supporting Your Argument 🧩

An opinion becomes much stronger when you explain **why**.

Compare:

> *Secondo me questa macchina è migliore.*

with:

> *Secondo me questa macchina è migliore perché consuma meno, costa meno da mantenere e ha più spazio.*

Now we have an argument.

You’ll learn how to use:

- *perché...*
- *infatti...*
- *per esempio...*
- *per questo motivo...*
- *inoltre...*
- *il punto è che...*
- *il problema è che...*

And most importantly:

how to organize your thoughts without starting every sentence with:

> *E poi... e poi... e poi...* 😄

---

# Opinion vs Fact 🧐

There is also an important difference between a **fact** and an **opinion**.

### Fact

> *Roma è la capitale d'Italia.*

Not much to argue about.

### Opinion

> *Roma è la città più bella d'Italia.*

Now we have a problem.

Someone from Florence has entered the chat. 😄

Useful expressions such as *secondo me* and *a mio parere* make it clear that you are expressing **your personal point of view**.

---

# Your Advanced Goal 🚀

By the end of Topic 19, you should be able to do more than say:

> *Mi piace.*

You should be able to say something like:

> *Secondo me è una buona idea perché è semplice ed economica. Capisco però che non tutti siano d'accordo.*

That is already a completely different level of Italian.

And if Geoff can defend his theory about half-liter espressos...

you can defend almost anything. ☕️😄
