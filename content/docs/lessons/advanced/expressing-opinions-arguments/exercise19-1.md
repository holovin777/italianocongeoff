# Exercise 19.1: Giving Your Opinion 💭✍️

You’ve learned several ways to express your opinion.

Now it’s time to use them.

And remember:

> *Secondo me...*

is useful.

But if every sentence starts with *secondo me*, eventually even Geoff will notice. 😄

---

## 1. Choose the Best Expression

Choose the most natural expression for each situation.

Use:

- **Secondo me**
- **A mio parere**
- **Per me**
- **Dal mio punto di vista**
- **Per quanto mi riguarda**
- **Mi sembra che**

### 1.

You are speaking casually with a friend.

> __________, questo film è troppo lungo.

---

### 2.

You are giving a more formal opinion during a meeting.

> __________, dovremmo considerare un'altra soluzione.

---

### 3.

You are talking about your personal availability.

> __________, possiamo partire anche domani mattina.

---

### 4.

You want to emphasize your own perspective.

> __________, il problema principale è la comunicazione.

---

### 5.

You are not completely sure that the shop is closed.

> __________ il negozio sia già chiuso.

---

### 6.

Someone asks whether a change is a problem for you personally.

> __________, non cambia niente.

## 6. Choose the Right Tone 🎭

Choose the expression that best fits the situation.

### Situation 1: Talking to a friend

Which sounds more natural?

A.

> A mio parere, sarebbe opportuno recarci al bar.

B.

> Secondo me, possiamo andare al bar.

---

### Situation 2: Professional meeting

Which sounds more appropriate?

A.

> A mio parere, sarebbe meglio rivedere il progetto.

B.

> Boh, per me non va.

---

### Situation 3: You are uncertain

Which expression is better?

A.

> Mi sembra che ci sia un problema.

B.

> È sicuramente un disastro totale.

---

### Situation 4: Your personal position

Which is better?

A.

> Per quanto mi riguarda, possiamo iniziare.

B.

> Tutti devono iniziare subito perché lo dico io.

Unless you are an emperor, choose carefully. 😄

## 7. Complete the Dialogue 💬

Complete the dialogue using suitable expressions from Lesson 19.1.
